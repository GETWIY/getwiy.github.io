01  The difference

Chosen for the language, not translated into it.

A Tamil, Telugu or Assamese reader gets quotes picked for that language, in that script, with a font that suits it — not machine translation of English. The interface exists in 22 languages, every string checked for parity.

02  Inside

What the app does

Getwiy Spark

A daily promise you make to yourself, renewed by tapping, with a streak of the days you stayed true. Not a habit tracker bolted on — it is the reason the app exists in the shape it does.

Dual-Language Experience

Read the same quote in two languages at once. For anyone who thinks in one language and reads in another, this is not a novelty.

Multi-Language Mixed Feed

One feed drawing from several languages together, instead of forcing you to pick a single one and stay there.

Main Screen Customization

Font, size, colour, italics, background, glow. The quote screen belongs to the person reading it, not to the developer.

Share as Image

Every quote becomes a 9:16 card, ready for a status or a story.

Write your own quotes

The app is not one-way. Your own insights are just as valuable as those of great thinkers.

05  Privacy

The app makes no network requests.

Not for content, not for fonts, not for analytics. Every quote, font and image ships inside the app itself. That is the architecture, not a promise — there is no server for your reading to travel to.

There are no ads and no tracking, and no mandatory account is needed. Uninstalling removes everything.
