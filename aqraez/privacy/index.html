This Privacy Policy explains how Aqraez ("the App", "we", "us"), developed under the name Getwiy, handles information when you use it. Please read this policy carefully. By using Aqraez, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Overview

Aqraez is designed to function entirely on your own device. It does not require you to create an account or sign in, and this version of the App makes no network requests of any kind — not for content, not for fonts, not for analytics. Every quote, font and image it uses is bundled inside the App itself. We operate no servers that store your personal data, and we integrate no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs into the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Profile details: your name, your age, gender, favourite authors, and preferred quote categories, entered during setup or in your Profile
  • A profile photo or custom theme photo, only if you choose to select one
  • Quotes you write yourself within the App
  • Your language, theme, and app icon preferences

Every setup step that asks for information can be skipped. If you skip a step, nothing from it is stored. You can also change or clear any of these later from Settings.

2.2 Information generated by using the App

  • Quotes you mark as favourites
  • Your daily usage streak and the dates you opened the App
  • Your Getwiy Spark promise history and streak
  • Achievement/badge progress
  • Your daily reminder time, if you set one, and whether achievement and streak notifications are switched on

Reminders are off until you set one. You can turn a reminder off again at any time on the Daily Reminder screen, and switch achievement or streak notifications on or off under Settings > Notifications.

2.3 Information we do not collect

We do not collect precise location, contacts, browsing history, device identifiers for advertising, or any information through analytics or crash-reporting services, because the App does not include any such services.

3. Permissions We Request

Android requires apps to explicitly ask for certain permissions. Here is what Aqraez requests and why:

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Used for three separate things, each of which you control on its own: your daily quote reminder, a streak reminder if you have not opened the App that day, and a message when you unlock an achievement. The daily reminder is off until you set one; the other two are switched on and off under Settings > Notifications. You can also decline or revoke the permission itself at any time in your device settings, without affecting the rest of the App.

Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)

Used so your daily reminder arrives close to the exact time you chose, rather than an approximate time. If this permission is not granted, reminders will still work but may arrive within a wider time window.

Boot Notification (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)

Used only to re-schedule your reminders after you restart your phone, so that a reminder you set does not silently stop working. This gives the App no additional access to your device.

Photos / Camera

Requested only at the moment you choose to set a profile photo or a custom background photo. Aqraez cannot access your photos at any other time, and the selected photo is stored only on your device.

Saving Images to Your Gallery

When you use the Save/Download feature on a quote image, Aqraez writes that newly created image directly into your device’s photo gallery. This uses Android’s standard "add a photo" capability, which is separate from — and narrower than — permission to read your existing photos. Aqraez does not gain any ability to browse, view, or access photos already on your device through this feature, and the saved image never leaves your device.

4. How We Use Your Information

Everything listed above is used solely to run the App the way you’ve configured it — showing your favourites, sending your reminder, tracking your streak, personalising your feed, and remembering your settings. We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond making the App work for you.

5. How We Store and Protect Your Information

All information described in this policy is stored locally on your device using standard Android app storage. It is not transmitted to Getwiy, and there is no Getwiy-operated server where this data is held — which means there is no central database of user data that could be exposed in a data breach on our end. The security of data on your device also depends on your own device’s security (such as a screen lock).

6. Third-Party Services

Aqraez does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. The only third-party interaction is when you actively choose to share a quote: at that point, the App hands your content to your device’s built-in share function (see Section 7). We do not embed any third-party tracking code.

7. Sharing Content

If you use the Share feature, Aqraez passes your chosen quote or quote image to whichever app you select (for example WhatsApp, Instagram, or Messages) through your device’s own share function. Getwiy does not see, store, or have access to what you share or who you share it with — that exchange happens directly between your device and the app you chose.

8. Notifications

Your daily reminder and achievement notifications are scheduled and shown directly by your device’s operating system. They are not sent from, or logged by, any server.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Because your information is stored only on your device, you are always in direct control of it:

  • Access: you can view everything you’ve entered directly within the App at any time
  • Correction: you can edit your profile, quotes, and preferences at any time in Settings
  • Deletion: uninstalling the App permanently deletes all locally stored data described in this policy
  • Permissions: you can review or revoke any permission (notifications, photos, etc.) at any time in your device’s App Settings

10. Data Retention

Since data is stored only on your device, it remains there for as long as the App is installed, and is removed automatically when you uninstall the App or clear its data through your device Settings.

11. International Users

Aqraez is available worldwide. For data protection laws that require a named data controller — including the EU/EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act — that controller is Getwiy, the developer identified at the end of this policy. In practice, because the App transmits nothing off your device, we do not receive, store or process your personal data on any server, and there is no cross-border transfer of it by us. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California, Brazil, or any other region with specific data protection rules, the rights those laws give you — access, correction, deletion, portability — are exercised directly on your own device, as described in Section 9, because that is the only place your data exists.
